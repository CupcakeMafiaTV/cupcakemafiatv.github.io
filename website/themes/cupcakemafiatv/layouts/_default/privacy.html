Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

This site (cupcakemafiatv.com) is my personal streaming and content-creation site. This page explains what data gets collected when you visit, and how it's used.

Analytics

Optional Google Analytics helps me understand which pages people visit. It loads only if you choose to enable it below, or previously accepted it. Your preference is saved in this browser. You can turn it off here at any time.

Live Stats & Embeds

The site displays public channel statistics and recent content from Twitch, YouTube, Discord, Riot Games, OP.GG, and Blizzard Entertainment. Most stats are retrieved through my server-side API and cached; the Charity page also requests public donation totals from Extra Life. These requests do not give me access to your social accounts.

Third-party content

Twitch players and thumbnails, and YouTube thumbnails and players, connect your browser directly to those providers. Requests can include your IP address, browser information, and referring site. Embedded players may use cookies or other identifiers under the providers' own policies, independently of the optional analytics setting above. Character artwork also connects directly to Blizzard’s image servers. See Twitch's privacy notice and Google's privacy policy, and Blizzard's legal and privacy information.

Browser storage and hosting

This site uses local browser storage for your analytics preference, cached stats and video details, recently shown clips, and game scores or sound preferences. You can clear these through your browser's site-data settings. GitHub Pages serves the site and Vercel serves its API; these services receive normal request information, including your IP address, to deliver and operate the site.

Contact

This site doesn't have sign-ups, accounts, or forms that collect personal information. If you email me directly at cupcakemafiatvgaming@gmail.com, that message (and your email address) is handled the same as any other email I receive—it isn't added to any mailing list or shared with anyone else.

Questions

If you have any questions about this policy, reach out at cupcakemafiatvgaming@gmail.com.
