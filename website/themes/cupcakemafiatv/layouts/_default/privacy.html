Privacy Policy

Last updated August 28, 2026

This site (cupcakemafiatv.com) is my personal streaming and content-creation site. This page explains what data gets collected when you visit, and how it's used.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand overall traffic—things like which pages get visited and roughly how many people are viewing the site. Google Analytics only loads after you accept the cookie banner shown on your first visit; if you decline, it never loads. Google Analytics may set cookies in your browser to do this. It does not collect your name, email, or any other information that identifies you personally. You can change your choice anytime by clearing this site's data in your browser, or opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all sites using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Live Stats & Embeds

Pages on this site display live public stats (follower counts, live/offline status, recent videos, League rank) pulled from the Twitch, YouTube, Discord, and Riot Games APIs. These are read-only public numbers about my own channel—no visitor data is sent to or collected from these third parties as part of that.

Contact

This site doesn't have sign-ups, accounts, or forms that collect personal information. If you email me directly at cupcakemafiatvgaming@gmail.com, that message (and your email address) is handled the same as any other email I receive—it isn't added to any mailing list or shared with anyone else.

Questions

If you have any questions about this policy, reach out at cupcakemafiatvgaming@gmail.com.
