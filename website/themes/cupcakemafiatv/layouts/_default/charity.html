Extra Life

Gaming for a good cause—one marathon stream at a time.

Every year for Extra Life Game Day, my friends and I go live for a 24-hour stream to support CHEO and the IWK Foundation. By hour 24, we're making complete fools of ourselves, but it's all to raise vital funds for sick and injured kids in our community.

100% of donations go directly to their care. If you've ever enjoyed hanging out in chat, this is the one stream a year where our community turns that fun into real, life-changing support.
