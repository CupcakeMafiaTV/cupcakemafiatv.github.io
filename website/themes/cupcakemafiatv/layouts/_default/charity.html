Extra Life

Gaming for a good cause, one marathon stream at a time. Every year on Extra Life Game Day, I run a 24 hour stream with friends in support of CHEO (Children's Hospital of Eastern Ontario) and IWK Health (the IWK Foundation). Twenty-four hours in, we're making complete fools of ourselves, all for a good cause and to raise donations. 100% of what's raised through my page goes straight to their care. If you've ever had fun in my chat, this is the one stream a year where that fun turns into something that actually helps sick and injured kids in our community.
