About Me

Hello, I’m CupcakeMafiaTV (Cody), a League of Legends content creator and variety streamer from Ontario, Canada. I’ve been streaming since 2016, sharing ranked climbs, community games, and whatever adventure catches my attention next. Mid lane is home, mages are my comfort pick, and my solo queue peak is Master 96 LP. League is a big part of the channel, but so are the people who make it fun. Whether we’re chasing the next rank, jumping into World of Warcraft, or hanging out for a community night, I’m here for good games and good company.

The Story Behind the Name

My name mixes fierce and sweet: “Mafia” comes from my love of mobsters, Wacky Races, and the Ant Hill Mob. “Cupcake” softens the gangster vibe, and “TV” was, in my best Abe Simpson voice, the style at the time.

My Gaming Journey

My gaming journey began with Theme Park (1994), followed by World of Warcraft, Warcraft III DotA, and Heroes of Newerth before I found League near the end of Season 2. I main mid lane for its mages and ranged champions, inspired by Bjergsen, xPeke, and the GOAT, Faker. Along the way, I’ve faced players including Quad and Tyler1.

Outside of the Rift

Away from ranked, I collect wrestling figures, Funko POPs, retro games, vinyl records, and anything else I can get my hands on. I also love Scott Pilgrim vs. The World, WWE, and DC Comics—especially Batman’s universe, Punchline, Teen Titans, Harley Quinn, and Poison Ivy.
