About Me

Hello, I'm CupcakeMafiaTV (Cody)!

I'm a streamer and League of Legends content creator. I first hit "Go Live" back in 2016 and streamed very inconsistently, but these past few years are where I really fell in love with the stream and content creation grind. It's been so amazing getting to interact and game with such incredible people!

The name "CupcakeMafia" came from wanting a gamertag that felt both fierce and “sweet” at the same time. I've always loved the mobster aesthetic, heavily inspired by my favorite childhood cartoon, Hanna-Barbera's Wacky Races, and its iconic Ant Hill Mob faction. To balance the gangster vibe with an item that everybody loves, I threw the term "Cupcake" into the mix.

The very first game I ever played was the 1994 classic Theme Park. From there, I lost years diving headfirst into World of Warcraft, which naturally led me down the custom game rabbit hole of Warcraft III DotA. That addiction kicked off my MOBA journey through Heroes of Newerth and eventually brought me to League of Legends starting all the way back in Season 2. I've always mained mid lane due to my love of ranged and mage champions, looking up to legends like Bjergsen, xPeke, and the one true GOAT, Faker.

When I'm not grinding ranked, I'm an avid collector hunting down rare wrestling action figures, Funko POPs, retro video games, vinyl records and anything else that I can get my grubby little hands on. I'm also a massive fan of Scott Pilgrim vs. The World, DC Comics (especially the Batman Universe), and my favorite chaos duo: Punchline and Harley Quinn.
