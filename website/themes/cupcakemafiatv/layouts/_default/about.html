About Me

Hey, I'm Cody — a streamer and content creator from Canada focused on League of Legends, grinding ranked and climbing the ladder one game at a time. I've been streaming on and off since 2016, but I've been at it consistently since 2024, finally treating it like something worth showing up for every week instead of a random hobby. By day I'm just your average computer technician, but by night I'm your much inferior trashcan teammate that always seems to let you down within competitive games.

Beyond streaming, I'm a massive collector of Funko POPs and nerdy memorabilia, always on the hunt for my next sought-after treasure to add to the collection. If you want to know my favorite thing in the world, it's Scott Pilgrim vs. The World — give me an excuse and I will talk your ear off about it. Some of my other favorite fandoms include DC Comics and the Batman Universe (especially Punchline & Harley Quinn), WWE/Wrestling, Pokémon, Yu-Gi-Oh & Digimon.
