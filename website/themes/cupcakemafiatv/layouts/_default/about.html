About Me

Hello! I'm Cody, a League of Legends streamer from Canada. In between countlessly quoting The Simpsons and my other favorite TV sitcoms, I'm typically found grinding ranked, chasing that next win. By day I'm just your average computer technician but by night I'm your much inferior trashcan teammate that always seems to let you down within competitive games. As someone who enjoys tinkering with technology, combining that skillset with my overall love of gaming has made streaming a perfect match.

Beyond streaming, I’m a massive collector of nerdy memorabilia, Funko POPs and trading cards and spend a lot of my free time looking for my next sought-after treasure to add to my collection. I’m a big fan of DC Comics and the Batman Universe especially Punchline & Harley Quinn and am most likely the only person that actually enjoyed the game Suicide Squad: Kill the Justice League. Some of my other all time favorite fandoms include Scott Pilgrim vs. The World, WWE/Wrestling, Pokémon, Yu-Gi-Oh & Digimon.
